Fence, Deck Installation and Repairs

Fence Services

  • Fence replacement and upgrades: Remove old fencing, repair posts and rails, and install new materials or upgrade to more durable options like pressure-treated wood or composite boards.

  • Repairs and maintenance: Fix broken posts, replace damaged panels, realign leaning sections, tighten hardware, and repair gate latches and hinges. Staining, sealing, and painting services to extend lifespan and maintain appearance.

    Deck Services

  • Repairs and structural reinforcement: Replace rotten joists, deck boards, and support posts; fix loose railings and stairs; reinforce framing to meet current load standards and local codes
